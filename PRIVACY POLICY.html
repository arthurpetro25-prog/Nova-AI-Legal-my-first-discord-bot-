# Nova AI — Privacy Policy **Effective date:** October 29, 2026 **Last updated:** October 29, 2026 ## 1. Introduction Nova AI ("Nova," "the Bot," "we," or "our") is an AI-powered Discord companion designed to provide friendly, conversational interactions. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you interact with Nova on Discord. By using the Bot, you acknowledge that your information may be processed as described below. ## 2. Information We Process Depending on how you use Nova, the Bot may process: - **Discord user ID:** Used to associate conversation context with a user. - **Discord channel ID:** Used to keep conversation context separate between channels. - **Messages:** Text you send using Nova's commands, such as `..chat`. - **AI-generated responses:** Responses generated by the AI model. - **Technical information:** Error information necessary to troubleshoot the Bot. Please do not send passwords, authentication tokens, financial information, or other sensitive personal information to Nova. ## 3. How Information Is Used Information is processed to: - Generate AI responses to user messages. - Maintain conversational context during interactions. - Provide commands such as `..help` and `..clear`. - Diagnose technical errors and maintain the Bot's functionality. Nova does not intentionally sell users' personal information. ## 4. Conversation Storage and Processing Nova uses Discord to receive messages and Ollama to generate AI responses. In the current implementation, conversation history is held temporarily in the Bot's memory while the Bot is running. The history is associated with a Discord user and channel. Conversation history can be cleared using `..clear`. In-memory history is also lost when the Bot process restarts. The current implementation does not intentionally save conversation history to a permanent database or local conversation archive. However, Discord, the hosting environment, and other relevant service providers may process or retain information according to their own policies and configurations. The AI model is configured to run through Ollama on the Bot host. If the Bot's configuration or hosting setup changes, the way messages are processed may change as well. ## 5. Third-Party Services Nova relies on third-party services, including: - **Discord:** For messaging, account identity, and Bot functionality. - **Ollama:** For running the AI model used to generate responses. These services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Nova does not control the data practices of third-party providers. ## 6. Data Sharing Nova does not intentionally sell or rent personal information. Information may be processed by Discord and the infrastructure used to operate the Bot. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law. ## 7. Data Retention and Deletion Conversation context is kept temporarily in memory for the duration of the Bot process unless cleared earlier. Users can clear their conversational context by using `..clear` in the relevant channel. This clears Nova's in-memory history for that user and channel; it does not delete messages already sent to Discord.
